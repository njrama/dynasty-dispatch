The State of the League

The rookie draft is done, and the league looks meaningfully different than it did a week ago. The top got top-heavier — rdfergus walked out with both Jeremiyah Love and Jahmyr Gibbs in the same backfield after two trades that nobody else in the league seems to have fully processed yet. Terra had a quietly excellent draft that got overshadowed by the Juwan Johnson thing. Bateman ran a one-man tight end exchange in real time. And off the field, the league voted to expand bench, IR, and taxi slots — a vote for long-term roster building, which is either great news or bad news depending on whether you log in. Onward.
