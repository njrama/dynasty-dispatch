The State of the League

Week 1 of Year 2 offers a clean board and a familiar problem: six teams are 1-0, six are 0-1, and the standings tell you almost nothing yet. What they do tell you is that the defending champ is already 0-1 after A.J. Brown walked off with a high ankle sprain, the reigning last-place finisher managed to lose by 46 while trading away another premium asset, and 11dhall — who reportedly hasn't logged in since June — dropped 169 points in his opener. The preseason narrative said this was a two-tier league with rdfergus15 and jaytherulerx21 on top and everyone else scrambling. Week 1 mostly agreed, then threw in some noise to keep everyone honest.

Game of the Week

Terra 156.6, njramadan 151.4. The champ ran into a buzzsaw at exactly the wrong time. D'Andre Swift's three-touchdown afternoon and Justin Jefferson's 2-TD line gave Terra a lead he never quite surrendered, even as Bijan Robinson (31.3), Christian Watson (147 and 2), and Isaiah Likely (27.8 on Sunday night) kept clawing njramadan back into it. The margin was 5.2. The AJ Brown injury, which will cost njramadan his WR1 for six weeks, was the loudest thing to happen all weekend and it barely showed up in the box score. Terra spent last year making trades that his league mates are still talking about; this week he took down the defending champ. The roster underneath still has to prove it can contend, but for now, the upset's on the board and njramadan's the one nursing the injury report.

37.3
